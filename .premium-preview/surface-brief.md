# Premium dashboard preview

Mode: Operate. Scope: isolated approval prototype for the authenticated CRM overview. No production route, component, or stylesheet may be changed.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A practice dashboard should feel as exact as brass set into white stone: quiet at first glance, unmistakably considered up close. It refuses the usual luxury-dashboard costume of black marble, gradients, oversized serif headlines, and gold on every control.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm white stone, pure white working surfaces, carbon ink, and restrained champagne-gold inlay. Tight eight-pixel cards, calm tabular figures, clear hairlines, and one architectural gold rail. Status red and green remain semantic and rare.

**STORY:** A partner opens the CRM, reads overdue money and statutory exposure first, then acts on the most urgent assignment. The product remains a working tool for long sessions, with realistic practice data and role-aware destinations.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A labelled 240px open-plane sidebar frames a fluid dashboard. Collections leads in a wide working surface; the seven-day statutory runway forms the signature gold inlay beside it. A compact attention queue and client receivables complete the first screen. New assignment is the single primary action.

**FORM:** Code-led Operate surface. Architectural brass inlay was the fourth grounded direction and the assigned build candidate. Taste basis: Achromatic ledger, seed 2139705747, with the shell redrawn twice because this CRM has too many destinations for an icon rail or top-only navigation. Impeccable reference: seed:b4cadec2726a3910.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance. For this approval-only preview, production documentation is intentionally deferred until the user approves a direction.
