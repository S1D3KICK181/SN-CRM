# SN Practice CRM

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Partners, managers, finance staff, associates, trainees, and support staff at SN & Associates. They use the CRM throughout the working day to coordinate client work, statutory deadlines, documents, billing, collections, and internal review.

## Product Purpose

SN Practice CRM gives an Indian accounting practice one operational view of its clients, engagements, assignments, tasks, documents, invoices, receipts, and follow-ups. Success means urgent work and overdue money are immediately visible, while the next action is clear for each role.

## Positioning

The product joins practice delivery and collections in the same client record, with Indian financial-year, currency, date, and statutory-deadline conventions built into the workflow.

## Operating Context

The CRM is used during long desktop sessions and quick mobile checks. Work is organized around clients, services, assignments, internal and statutory due dates, requested documents, review states, invoices, receipts, and payment allocations. Founders and management need a practice-wide view; staff need assigned work and follow-ups.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- TanStack Start frontend connected to Lovable Cloud through Supabase.
- Client-side Supabase access with row-level security as the permission layer.
- Role-aware navigation and data visibility.
- Indian formats: INR, DD/MM/YYYY, financial-year labels, and Asia/Kolkata.
- Database-generated business codes and allocation-driven invoice balances.
- A browser-local demo backend exists for preview and evaluation.
- Existing routes, behavior, data relationships, and permissions must survive any visual redesign.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named SN Practice CRM and belongs to SN & Associates. The user has requested a supremely premium visual direction built around white and restrained gold. This is a visual constraint, not permission to make the interface ornamental or reduce operational clarity.

## Evidence on Hand

The working codebase contains realistic demo clients, staff roles, assignments, tasks, services, invoices, receipts, documents, reports, and settings. No customer testimonials, awards, or commercial performance claims are available and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make deadlines and overdue money legible before decoration.
- Keep every frequent action one clear step from the data that prompts it.
- Preserve financial accuracy, role boundaries, and honest zero or missing states.
- Make long working sessions calm, fast, and trustworthy.
- Let the identity feel premium through proportion, typography, material restraint, and detail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface must remain keyboard operable, maintain visible focus, meet readable contrast, support reduced motion, and retain usable touch targets on mobile.
